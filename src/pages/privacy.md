Heartland Transpersonal Alliance uses this website to share organizational information, news, board application access,
and contact pathways.

## Information We Receive

We may receive information you choose to send to us, such as your name, email address, inquiry topic, message, board
application responses, or direct email correspondence.

## How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, coordinate nonprofit programs, review board applications, and
maintain organizational records.

## Third-Party Services

This site may link to or embed third-party services, including Google Forms for board applications, external initiative
websites, Facebook, GitHub, RSS readers, and email clients. Those services have their own privacy practices.

## Analytics And Cookies

The site is configured without a Google Analytics ID. Basic browser or hosting logs may still be created by infrastructure
providers as part of normal website delivery.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to [info@psychedelickc.org](mailto:info@psychedelickc.org).
