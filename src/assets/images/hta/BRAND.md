# HTA web identity

The web mark is a hand-built SVG adaptation of **HTA LOGO, page 7** in the supplied [Canva folder](https://canva.link/d00x9bf5xlx9dev), design `DAG7D3_FkYk`. It preserves the monochrome HTA lettering and split angular A, without the small raster tagline. It is not an official Canva vector export. The organization name is accessible live text in the shared Logo component. Canva originals remain unchanged.

The favicon uses the angular A. Social artwork is rendered from local SVG at 1200 × 630. Typography is locally bundled Space Grotesk and Source Sans 3, distributed by Fontsource under their included OFL licenses. Colors are centralized in CustomStyles.astro.
